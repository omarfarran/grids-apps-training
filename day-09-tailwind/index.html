A few things you might be wondering

Clear answers. Less guesswork.

Can I explore Forma without an account?

Yes. The sample workspace is open to explore. Follow “Workspace” in the navigation to see the dashboard and its related pages.

How does the sample pricing work?

Focus includes Projects for $12 per person monthly. Studio adds Teamspace for a total of $21. Complete adds Insights for a total of $39. These prices are illustrative; checkout and subscriptions are not available.

Can I start with one tool?

That is the idea behind the product family: Projects for planning, Teamspace for collaboration, and Insights for reporting. Explore each module above.

Does the workspace work on a small screen?

The navigation and cards adapt to your screen. Data tables keep every column and can be scrolled sideways when space is limited.

Will the demo save anything I type?

No. Forms demonstrate their appearance and native controls only. Save and sign-in actions are disabled. Use sample information rather than real account details.
